Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. During high-volume periods (holidays, sales events), processing may take up to 3 business days.

You'll receive an email confirmation when your order ships, including tracking information.

Package Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. Use this to track your package:

  1. Check your email for the shipping confirmation
  2. Click the tracking link or visit our order tracking page
  3. Enter your order number and email/zip code
  4. View real-time updates on your package location

Delivery Issues

Package Not Delivered

If your package shows as delivered but you haven't received it:

  • Check with neighbors and building management
  • Look for a delivery notice
  • Wait 24 hours (sometimes carriers scan early)
  • Contact us if still not found

Damaged Package

If your package arrives damaged:

  • Take photos of the damage
  • Keep all packaging materials
  • Contact us within 48 hours
  • We'll arrange a replacement or refund

Special Delivery Instructions

You can add special delivery instructions during checkout:

  • "Leave at back door"
  • "Ring doorbell twice"
  • "Deliver to apartment office"
  • "Call upon arrival"

Note: Carriers may not always be able to follow special instructions.

Common Questions

Can I change my shipping address after ordering?

Address changes can be made within 24 hours of order placement. After this time, please contact customer service immediately.

Do you ship to PO Boxes?

Yes, we ship to PO Boxes via USPS. However, expedited shipping options may not be available.

How are shipping costs calculated?

Shipping costs are based on package weight, dimensions, destination, and selected shipping speed.

What happens if I'm not home when my package arrives?

Carriers will typically leave the package at your door or with a neighbor. You can also provide delivery instructions during checkout.
