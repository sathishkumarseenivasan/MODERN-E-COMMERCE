Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Information We Collect

At Modern Store, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. We collect information to provide better services and enhance your shopping experience.

Personal Information

  • Name and contact information (email, phone, address)
  • Payment details (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
  • Shipping and billing addresses
  • Account credentials (username, password)

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address and browser information
  • Device information and operating system
  • Browsing behavior and purchase history
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Personalize your shopping experience
  • Send order updates and marketing communications
  • Improve our products and services
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate our business (payment processors, shipping companies)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your explicit consent

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including SSL encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.
