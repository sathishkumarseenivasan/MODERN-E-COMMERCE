Returns & Refunds

Our hassle-free return policy ensures you can shop with confidence

Return Policy

30-Day Return Window

Return unused items in original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Eligible for Return

  • Unused items in original packaging
  • Items with all tags and accessories
  • Products in resalable condition
  • Items within 30 days of delivery

Non-Returnable Items

  • Personal care items
  • Customized or personalized products
  • Perishable goods
  • Final sale items
  • Digital downloads

How to Return an Item

1

Initiate Your Return

Log into your account and go to "Order History" to start the return process, or contact our customer service team.

2

Print Return Label

We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label. Print it and attach it to your package.

3

Package Your Item

Place the item in its original packaging with all tags and accessories. Include the return form if provided.

4

Ship the Package

Drop off the package at any authorized shipping location. Keep your tracking receipt until your refund is processed.

5

Receive Your Refund

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days.

Refund Process

Refund Timeline

  • Return Received: We'll email you when your return arrives at our facility
  • Inspection (1-2 days): Our team inspects the returned item
  • Refund Processing (3-5 days): Refund is issued to your original payment method
  • Bank Processing (3-5 days): Your bank processes the refund

Total time: 7-12 business days from when we receive your return

Refund Methods

  • Credit/Debit Card: Refunded to original card
  • PayPal: Refunded to PayPal account
  • Store Credit: Available immediately (optional)
  • Exchange: Processed as new order (optional)

Return Costs

Free Returns

  • Defective items
  • Wrong items shipped
  • Items damaged during shipping
  • Our error in any way

Return Fees May Apply

  • Customer preference returns: $5.99
  • Restocking fee for some items: 10%
  • Original shipping costs (if free shipping was used)

Exchanges

Want a different size, color, or style? We make exchanges easy!

Exchange Process

  1. Initiate return as described above
  2. Select "Exchange" as your return reason
  3. Choose your replacement item
  4. We'll ship your new item once the return is processed
  5. Expedited shipping available for exchanges

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, we'll make it right immediately.

What to Do:

  • Take photos of the damage/defect
  • Keep all packaging materials
  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Choose between replacement or refund

We'll cover all return shipping costs for damaged/defective items.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I lost my return label?

Contact our customer service team and we'll resend your return label immediately.

Can I return an item I received as a gift?

Yes! Gift recipients can return items for store credit or exchange. Contact us with the order number.

How do I track my return?

Use the tracking number on your return label to monitor your package back to our facility.

What if my return is lost in transit?

File a claim with the shipping carrier using your tracking number. We'll assist with the process.

Need Help with a Return?

Our customer service team is ready to assist with any return questions or concerns.
